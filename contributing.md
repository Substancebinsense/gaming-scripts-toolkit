## 🌟 About

**Gaming Scripts Toolkit** is a curated collection of automation scripts, macros, and utilities designed to **level up your gaming experience**. From quality-of-life tweaks to streamer overlays, from modding helpers to performance optimizations — everything is organized, documented, and ready to run.

Built by gamers, for gamers. No fluff, no bloat — just tools that work.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** All scripts are for **personal, educational & single-player use only**. Do not use in competitive multiplayer games — that violates most ToS. Always respect game publishers' rules and other players. Use responsibly.
